\chapter{Control channel}


To demonstrate that the procedure used to discriminate correctly reconstructed signal events from mis-reconstructed ones and described in \ref{sec:wronglyBtag}, 
and more generally that the adopted analysis strategy is well developed, a dedicated control sample,  i.e. a channel with similar properties to the signal,
is employed.  The flavor correlated $B^+ \rightarrow D^0 X$ decay, due to the much larger statistics and its current relatively precise measured value ($79 \pm 4 \%$  PDG ref)
 represents the closest and most suited reference mode. Any residual discrepancy between data and simulation in this channel would
 suggest a potential over-tuning of the analysis procedure to the simulated sample, or the
 presence of other methodological biases. Owing to its large event yield and well-established
 branching fraction, this control channel provides a robust benchmark for validating the
 analysis strategy and reinforcing confidence in the chosen methodology.\\

\section{Analysis setup} 
Same BASF2 release and FEI versions were used as for the $B \rightarrow \Lambda_c$ analysis.
\subsection{Dataset used}

   For this analysis the amount of data and Monte Carlo simulated data used was restricted to the SVD2 period: 
   experiments ranging from 31 to 65. This choice was made to save processing time, anyway most of the $B\bar{B}$ meson pairs were produced in this range of experiments 
   (620 $\times 10^6$ out of almost 800 $\times 10^6$ ).
   
   \subsection{Event selection and reconstruction}
   
   The approach used for the inclusive decays reconstruction of $B^+ \rightarrow D^0 X$ events is the same adopted for the $B \rightarrow \Lambda_c$ analysis:
   \begin{itemize} 
\item charged $B_{tag}$ candidates are reconstructed hadronically by FEI
\item only candidates with highest $Signal Probability$ are retained
\item "crossfeed-vetoeing" of $B^0\bar{B^0}$ events 
\item in the \textit{rest of event} charged tracks are selected in order to reconstruct $D^0$ candidates in the  $D^0 \rightarrow K^+ \pi^-$ decay mode.
   \end{itemize} 

  % The same FEI training was used, 
  Since among the decay chains used by the FEI to reconstruct  $B$ meson candidates the $B^+ \rightarrow D^0$ decay is also present,
   the decay used in our analysis to reconstruct the signal ($D^0 \rightarrow K^+ \pi^-$) must be excluded from the FEI decay chains.\\
   The preliminary selection criteria applied to the tag-side $B$ meson candidates are the same as the ones used in the $B \rightarrow \Lambda_c$ analysis \cref{sec:BtagReco}. \\
   \noindent In the \textit{rest of event} (ROE) of the reconstructed $B_{tag}$ meson, to select $D^0 \rightarrow K^+ \pi^-$ signal candidates, the following event selection criteria are applied:
   \begin{itemize}
   \item $dr <$ 2 cm and $|dz| <$ 4 cm
   \item $\frac{\mathcal{L}_{K}}{\mathcal{L_{K}}+\mathcal{L_{\pi}}} > 0.6$
   \end{itemize}
   For the $D^0 $ candidates a vertex fit is performed with \texttt{TreeFitter}, requiring it to converge.  If there are more than one $D^0$ combination, then the best candidate based on the $\chi^2$ probability is chosen. The $D^0$ signal region is defined to be $|M_{D^0}  - m_{D^0}| < $   30  MeV/$c^2$ 
   \newline \noindent ($\sim$ 3$\sigma$), where $m_{D^0 }$ is the nominal mass of $D^0$.\\

   \subsection{Signal selection optimization}\label{Sec:SigSelectionOpt}

   Following the same procedure as for the $B \rightarrow \Lambda_c$ analysis, the optimized selection cuts obtained for the event based ratio
   of the 2-nd to the 0-th order Fox-Wolfram moments, the $B_{tag}$ signal probability and the momentum of the $D^0$ candidates in the center of mass system are\footnote{illustrative plots can be found in Appendix}
   \begin{itemize}
    \item $foxWolframR2 <$ 0.3
    \item SignalProbability $>$ 0.004
    \item $p^{D^0 }_{CMS} > 1$ GeV/c$^2$
    \end{itemize}

     \subsection{Probability Density Functions (PDFs) for two dimensional fit}
     
     As already said the main goal of the control sample analysis is to ensure that the method used to extract the signal yields discriminating the correctly reconstructed from the misreconstructed signal events by fitting is valid.
     The reconstructed events in $M_{bc}$ are fitted with a Crystal Ball, the misreconstructed signal with a Novosibirsk function. Both type of events peak in the $D^0$ invariant mass as one can see from the figure below
  , and in both cases the invariant mass is fitted with a sum of three gaussians with a common mean.
     The fitted distribution of $M_{bc}$ and $M(\pi K)$ are shown in Fig. \ref{fig:chargedTotalSignal2Dfit} with signal MC sample.

     \begin{itemize}
         \item \textbf{generic} (charged $B$) background

     \noindent \textbf{Generic background}
     
     \noindent The generic background deriving from other $B^+B^-$ events presents a similar shape in $M_{bc}$: it is fitted again with a sum of Crystal Ball and Novosibirsk function. Instead the distribution in  the $D^0$ mass  is fitted with a sum of first order Polynomial function and a small gaussian peak, which is due to the small 
     amount of flavor anti-correlated $B^+ \rightarrow D^0$ reconstructed events (see Fig. \ref{fig:chargedControlD0_Generic_2DFit}). The total two-dimensional PDF is a product of the one-dimensional PDFs in $M_{bc}$ and $D^0$ mass:
     
     \begin{equation}
     P^{GenBkg}_{B,D^0}(M_{bc}, M(K \pi)) = [\Gamma_{CB}(M_{bc}) + \Gamma_{Nov}(M_{bc})] \times [\rho_{pol1}(M(K \pi)+ \rho_{G}(M(K \pi))
      \end{equation}

\subsection{2D Fit on Monte Carlo simulated data}\label{sec:2Dfit_chargedControlD0_onMC}
           
As in the $\BtoLambda$ study, five streams of Monte Carlo simulated data have been used to get values for the shaping parameters for the individidual components described in the previous section and the fit model is tested on 
an independent stream.\\
Unbinned maximum likelihood fit is performed on all six streams.
In the fits on the two-dimensional total distributions all the shaping parameters are kept fixed, except:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $\sigma_{G1}$: the width of the wider of the three Gaussian functions in $\rho_G(M(p K \pi))$
    \item $\sigma_{CB}$: width of the Crystal Ball describing the signal
  \end{itemize}

The width $\sigma^{generic}_{CB}$ of the Crystal Ball describing the generic background is expressed as funvtion of the signal $\sigma_{CB}$
as product $\sigma^{signal}_{CB} \cdot r_{\sigma}$, with $r_{\sigma}$ being the ratio of the two widths obtained from the five streams'sample fit.
As for the normalizations:
\begin{itemize}
  \item reconstructed and misreconstructed are obviously floated
  \item generic background is also floated
  \item crossfeed background normalization is instead estimated in the same way as in the $B_{tag} $ fit: its value is estimated through
  the ratio of between its contribution and misreconstructed signal events obtained from the five streams'sample fit ($N^{crossfeed} = N^{misreco} \cdot R^{(\frac{crossfeed}{misreco})}$)
  \item continuum background is obtained from the off-resonance scaling procedure as usual
\end{itemize}

\section{Results on Monte Carlo simulated data}
The branching ratio is calculated with same ingredients as for the $B \rightarrow \Lambda_c$  analysis:
\begin{itemize}
  \item reconstructed signal yield in the two dimensional fit
  \item reconstructed signal yield in the $M_{bc}$ fit of the tagged $B$ mesons, which provides the normalization
  \item $\epsilon_{D^0}$: $D^0$ reconstruction efficiency 
  \item $\frac{\epsilon^{+}_{FEI}}{ \epsilon^{+}_{FEI,  sig }}$, i.e. the ratio of the FEI efficiencies
  \item the branching fraction for the decay mode used to rectonstruct the signal: $\mathcal{B}(D^0 \rightarrow K^+ \pi^-) = 3.8\% $ in Belle DECAY.DEC table
